## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an optimistic, high-velocity, and intellectually vibrant atmosphere tailored for the modern academic ecosystem. Centered on the brand ethos *"Lebih Banyak Koneksi, Lebih Banyak Berkolaborasi,"* the interface bridges cross-faculty networking, campus hackathons, study clusters, and student venture incubation.

The visual direction merges **Modern Functional Minimalism** with **Tactile Glass-Tinged Elevation**. The aesthetic is clean, light, and airy—steering away from sterile corporate platforms in favor of an energetic digital campus courtyard. Visual affordances prioritize effortless scannability, interactive momentum, and frictionless discovery. Micro-interactions express real-time campus presence through luminous indicators, buoyant pill badges, and structured modular cards that elevate peer projects and study sessions into compelling visual artifacts.

## Colors

The color architecture is built strictly for a pristine light mode with high atmospheric clarity and focused academic energy.

- **Primary (`#4F46E5` Vibrant Indigo):** Represents network cohesion, analytical intelligence, and institutional momentum. Used for primary CTAs, key focus rings, active navigation items, and highlighted metric indicators. Complementary states:
  - Deep Anchor (`#4338CA`) for hover/pressed interactions.
  - Electric Tinge (`#6366F1`) for link accents, gradient terminals, and subtle tinted badge backgrounds.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` Emerald Teal):** Denotes active status, live collaboration sessions, verified students, and thriving campus hotspots. Signals vitality and real-time synchronization. Secondary accent tier `#059669` is reserved for accessible high-contrast text on bright backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` Amber / `#F97316` Coral):** Dedicated to gamification, XP points, leaderboards, faculty milestone badges, and urgent project recruitment deadlines.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - Base Canvas (`#F8FAFC` Slate 50): Provides a luminous, eye-strain-reducing backdrop.
  - Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` Pure White): Crisp contrast against the soft base canvas.
  - Structural Hairlines (`#E2E8F0` Slate 200 / `#CBD5E1` Slate 300): Grounding borders that maintain structural clarity.
  - Text Hierarchy: Deep Ink Slate (`#0F172A` Slate 900) for primary headlines, `#475569` (Slate 600) for secondary body copy, and `#94A3B8` (Slate 400) for placeholder metadata.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** universally across display, body, and label roles. Its geometric underpinnings combined with warm, humanist apertures project contemporary digital confidence without institutional stiffness.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set with tighter negative letter-spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) and assertive weights (700/800) to anchor modular card dashboards and hero invitation copy.
- **Body Architecture:** Set with comfortable line height multiples (1.5x–1.6x) in neutral slate tones (`#334155` to `#475569`) to guarantee fatigue-free reading during prolonged project brief reviews and research discussions.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Rendered with elevated weights (600/700) and slight tracking expansion for tags, live indicators, campus department markers, and navigation controls.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptable **12-column responsive fluid grid** anchored by an 8pt base unit. 

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and max-width containers centered at `1280px` (`80rem`). Outer margin is `2rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, `1.5rem` outer canvas padding. Secondary utility rails fold into slide-over panels or horizontal carousels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1rem` screen margin. Complex analytical grids collapse into vertical linear cards. Floating actions (quick team assembly, search) lock into fixed bottom sheets and nav bars.

Horizontal and vertical rhythm strictly utilizes the `space-*` tokens:
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for icon-to-label couplings, avatar overlaps, and badge padding.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for interior component padding (standard cards, input paddings, list-item separations).
- Use `space-lg` (24px) and `space-xl` (40px) for separating major functional groups, dashboard canvas widgets, and campus activity sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of **tonal layering**, **1px crisp perimeter borders**, and **indigo/slate-tinted ambient diffusion**. Harsh, muddy drop-shadows are avoided in favor of luminous depth.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Modules, Search Bars):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, bounded by a subtle border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Active Filter Toggles, Floating Widgets):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Context Menus, Interactive Campus Map Overlays):** Surface `#FFFFFF` (or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`). Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Note the faint indigo tinting in high elevations to reinforce platform vibrancy.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an approachable, hyper-modern geometry using balanced curved forms:

- **Base Radius (`rounded`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Form fields, action buttons, table rows, and secondary widgets.
- **Card Radius (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`, 1rem - 1.5rem / 16px - 24px):** Project cards, community showcase tiles, and map preview viewports.
- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full`):** Category tags, role identifiers, status indicators, avatar counters, and search capsules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#4F46E5` background, white label, `0.5rem` border radius, padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`. Hover transitions to `#4338CA` with subtle elevation lift (`shadow-sm`).
- **Secondary / Soft:** Tinted indigo background (`#EEF2FF`), text `#4338CA`, borderless.
- **Outline:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text. Hover prompts `#F8FAFC` background and `#4F46E5` border accent.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full`), horizontal padding `0.75rem`, vertical padding `0.25rem`. Font `label-sm`.
- **Live Activity Hotspots:** Soft emerald background (`#ECFDF5`), `#059669` typography, accompanied by a dynamic `6px` emerald ping indicator.
- **Skill / Gamification Badges:** Soft amber background (`#FEF3C7`), text `#D97706` for XP/honors, accompanied by micro vector emblems.

### Inputs & Global Search Bars
- **Global Search Capsule:** Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, pill-rounded (`rounded-full`), inner left adornment with `#64748B` search icon, trailing keyboard command badge (`Ctrl+K` in slate fill). Focus state applies a dual-ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)` with border `#4F46E5`.
- **Form Inputs:** Height `42px`, `rounded-md`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, smooth transition to indigo focus outline.

### Cards & Campus Project Tiles
- Built with pure `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, and `1.25rem` (`rounded-xl`) corner radius.
- Padding: `1.25rem` (`20px`). Includes clear vertical separation between author metadata, role vacancy chips, and collaboration velocity bars.

### Avatar Stacks
- Overlapping concentric circles (`-space-x-2.5`).
- Bordered with a solid `2px` ring of pure `#FFFFFF` to maintain separation between student portraits.
- The trailing element features an indigo or slate counter circle (e.g., `+5`) rendered in `label-sm` weight.

### Live Pulse Indicators
- Consists of a dual-element anchor: an absolute inner circle (`6px`, `#10B981`) enveloped by an animated ping ring (`opacity-75`, scale 1.8x cycle) signaling real-time collaborative activity in rooms, hackathons, and study sessions.

### Interactive Map Previews
- Bounded inside `rounded-xl` containers with subtle inset borders.
- Map skin features muted, desaturated light topography with high-contrast, indigo-ringed pulsing beacons marking active campus study clusters and innovation labs.
